# kind: text (profile: handwriting)

## Read
Treat the focus as handwritten prose: transcribe it exactly, word for word. Keep line
breaks as ' / '.

## Reply
Answer in prose. Maths only if it genuinely helps.

## Pitfalls
- OCR transcriptions may carry small errors: read through them, do not echo them.
- Journaling entries want reflection, not a lecture: one sharp line beats three soft ones.
